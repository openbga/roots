How these details are used

  • These details print on reports issued from now on. Reports already issued keep what they printed.
  • A certificate marked for printing is printed only on reports issued within its recorded validity. Both dates are included, and a blank date sets no limit.
  • The application keeps no register of what a certificate covers and does not check results against any certificate.

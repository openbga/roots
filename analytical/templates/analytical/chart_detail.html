    {% endlist_results %} {% endpanel %} {% panel title="How the statistical bands are made, and what they are not" heading_id="band-notes-heading" id="band-notes" collapsible=True class="mb-3" %}
    • The bands are the mean plus or minus 2 and 3 sample standard deviations (n − 1 divisor, no bias correction) of the baseline points chosen below.
    • The chosen recent points are used as they are. Nothing checks that the process was in statistical control during them, so excursions in the baseline widen the bands.
    • The bands are recomputed every time this page opens. They are not fixed reference limits, and points inside the baseline are compared with bands they helped set.
    • Drawing bands only from {{ min_baseline }} or more points is this application's display threshold. EPA 815-R-05-004 section 7.2.8 (laboratory fortified blank recoveries: usually 20 to 30 results, 3 S limits, 2 S warning) and MARLAP Chapter 18 Procedure 18.1 (ideally at least 20 points) describe the common practice; neither makes it a requirement for these charts.
    • Using the same bands for blanks, matrix spikes or RPD is common practice, not something those passages state.

    The laboratory's configured limits alone decide QC status at approval and release. These charts are an aid for looking at trends.
